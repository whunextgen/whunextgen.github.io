Congratulations to **Enze Zhang** and **Xiyang Huang**, whose papers have been accepted to the main conference of **The 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2026)**.

## DITING: Evaluating Sentence-Level Cultural Meaning in Web Novel Translation

*Enze Zhang, Jiaying Wang, Mengxi Xiao, Jifei Liu, Ziyan Kuang, Rui Dong, Eric Dong, Sophia Ananiadou, Min Peng, Qianqian Xie*

DITING is the first comprehensive evaluation framework for web novel translation. It defines six dimensions of cultural and narrative fidelity, provides more than 18,000 expert-annotated Chinese–English sentence pairs, and introduces AgentEval, a multi-agent evaluator that simulates expert discussion and correlates more closely with human judgement than existing automatic metrics.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09116>
- Code and data: <https://github.com/WHUNextGen/DITING>

## SiMing-Bench: Evaluating Procedural Correctness from Continuous Interactions in Clinical Skill Videos

*Xiyang Huang, Jiawei Lin, Keying Wu, Jiaxin Huang, Kailai Yang, Renxiong Wei, Cheng Zeng, Jiayi Xiang, Ziyan Kuang, Min Peng, Qianqian Xie, Sophia Ananiadou*

SiMing-Bench is a benchmark for assessing whether multimodal language models can judge procedural correctness in clinical skill videos such as CPR and bag-mask ventilation against physician-established rubrics. It shows that current models struggle to track how ongoing interactions update the procedural state.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09037>
