On the afternoon of **April 1, 2026**, **Fan Zhang** visited the Center for Language and Information Research and gave a talk in Room A211 of the School of Artificial Intelligence.

Fan Zhang is a second-year PhD student at the University of Tokyo and a visiting student at MBZUAI and IFM, working on AI for Finance. Before his PhD he worked in quantitative research at fintech companies, brokerage research institutes and proprietary trading desks, focusing on multi-factor model development and risk management, including the application of Barra multi-factor models to risk management and portfolio optimisation.

![Talk](./assets/news-20260401-fan-zhang-talk-1.jpg)

The talk, framed as a path from quantitative research to AI for Finance, introduced two lines of work:

- **FinCards** converts fragmented financial disclosures into structured, comparable and verifiable information units (“cards”) to support reliable cross-document and cross-jurisdiction analysis.
- **FinReporting** uses AI to automatically extract and unify the three core financial statements from annual reports of US, Japanese and Chinese A-share listed companies, serving investment research and quantitative modelling.

![Discussion](./assets/news-20260401-fan-zhang-talk-2.jpg)

The talk was followed by a discussion with lab members working on financial large language models and evaluation. We thank Fan Zhang for sharing his work.
