On December 25, 2025, **Dr. Guojun Xiong**, a postdoctoral fellow in Computer Science at Harvard University, visited the Center for Language and Information Research for a **Qiming Forum** talk and discussion on structured reinforcement learning and sequential decision making under uncertainty.

## Using problem structure to improve decisions

The talk addressed complex sequential decisions under uncertainty and resource constraints. It examined how structured reinforcement learning can exploit the properties of a problem to improve sample efficiency and learning speed when information is incomplete and environments change.

Dr. Xiong's research spans online sequential decision making, stochastic optimisation and control, finite-time convergence and regret analysis for reinforcement learning, and decentralised optimisation and multi-agent reinforcement learning in networked decision systems. These questions connect algorithmic analysis with practical requirements for reliable decision support.
