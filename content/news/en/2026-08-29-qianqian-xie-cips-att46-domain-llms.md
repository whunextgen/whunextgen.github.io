The **21st CIPS Summer School and Advanced Technology Tutorial (CIPS ATT46)** took place in Beijing on August 28–29, 2026. Organised by the Chinese Information Processing Society of China, the tutorial focused on frontier technologies and innovative applications of large models and agents.

On **August 29, Prof. Qianqian Xie** of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence delivered a tutorial titled **“Domain Applications of Large Models”**. Drawing on healthcare and finance, she discussed the research questions that arise when general model capabilities are applied to professional tasks.

## Domain models and multi-agent problem solving

The tutorial covered methods for constructing and enhancing domain models, and approaches to solving professional tasks through multi-agent systems. It connected the organisation of domain knowledge and model capabilities with tasks that require multiple information sources and several reasoning steps.

Prof. Xie also discussed applications in representative domains. Healthcare and finance have their own knowledge requirements and workflows, making task-specific analysis of understanding, reasoning and reliability central to model development.

## Evaluation and application challenges

The evaluation of domain models and agents was another focus. The presentation connected model development, multi-agent mechanisms, applications and assessment, and considered future challenges in professional deployment.
