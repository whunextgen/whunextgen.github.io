On the afternoon of **May 18, 2026**, **Dr. Yankai Chen** visited the Center for Language and Information Research at the invitation of Prof. Qianqian Xie and gave a **Qiming Forum** talk in Room A228 titled **“Scaling Predictive Agentic Systems with Data Synthesis: from Single-Step Inference to Complex Decision-Making”**.

Dr. Chen is a postdoctoral associate at MBZUAI. He previously held postdoctoral positions at Cornell University and the Chinese University of Hong Kong, where he received his PhD in 2023, after a master's degree from the University of Hong Kong and a bachelor's degree from Nanjing University. His research is in human-centred AI and machine learning. He has published more than 50 papers at venues such as ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, KDD, WWW, ACL and SIGIR, including 22 first- or corresponding-author papers in CCF-A venues, and has interned at Amazon, Huawei Noah's Ark Lab and Microsoft Research Asia, with work deployed in Amazon's online personalisation engine.

Predictive AI systems, which estimate future outcomes, unknown knowledge or latent states from observed context, underpin recommendation, ranking, forecasting and risk assessment, yet their ability to generalise is bounded by the quantity, diversity and alignment of the training data. The talk presented a unified perspective on multiple forms of data synthesis and showed how different levels of synthesis help downstream predictive tasks, from single-step inference to complex decision-making. Across these settings, data synthesis acts not merely as an augmentation trick but as a core mechanism for improving generalisation, enabling scalability and expanding what predictive agentic systems can reliably do.

We thank Dr. Chen for the visit and the discussion with lab members that followed.
