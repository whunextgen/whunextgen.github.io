**MLNLP has named Prof. Qianqian Xie of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence in its second group of academic committee members for 2026.** The role connects the lab's research with an open community for machine learning and natural language processing.

## Supporting an academic community

MLNLP brings together university faculty, graduate students, industry researchers and learners in machine learning and NLP. Its academic activities promote the exchange of research and experience across academia, industry and those entering the field.

According to the announcement, academic committee members take part in the community's day-to-day work and the planning, organisation and delivery of academic activities. Prof. Xie's appointment provides another connection through which the lab can engage with current research and academic discussion.

## Research in NLP and domain AI

Prof. Xie's research spans natural language processing, medical AI and intelligent finance, including domain-model development, evaluation, reasoning and applications. Her academic service includes conference organisation, reviewing and shared evaluation tasks.

As a committee member, she will contribute to MLNLP's academic exchange and research-sharing activities.
