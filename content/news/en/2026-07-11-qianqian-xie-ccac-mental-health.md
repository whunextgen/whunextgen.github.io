The **sixth China Conference on Affective Computing (CCAC 2026)** opened in Wuhan on July 11, 2026. Organised by the CIPS Technical Committee on Affective Computing, the conference brought together research on affective-computing theory, methods and applications.

That afternoon, **Prof. Qianqian Xie** of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence took part in the **Mental Health Computing Forum**, sharing the team's research on trustworthy mental health computing.

## Trustworthy computing for mental health tasks

Mental health tasks draw on language, emotional signals and domain knowledge. Understanding these sources of information and assessing the reasoning behind model judgments are continuing questions for this research area.

Prof. Xie shared research on trustworthy mental health computing and joined the forum's exchange on technical approaches and applications. These themes connect with the lab's work on mental health NLP, language-model reasoning and domain evaluation.

## Exchange within the research community

CCAC 2026 included keynote talks, frontier-trends discussions, parallel thematic forums and poster sessions. The Mental Health Computing Forum provided a setting for researchers to discuss task requirements, methods and applications across affective computing and mental health research.
