On January 18, 2026, the **ACL SIG-FinTech × TFAI Webinar Series** held its first session. The series is a biweekly online seminar on benchmarks and models for financial AI, organised by **The Fin AI** together with partner centres including Yale University, the National Centre for Text Mining (NaCTeM, University of Manchester) and MBZUAI.

**Prof. Qianqian Xie** serves as Secretary of the ACL Special Interest Group on Financial Technology (SIG-FinTech). CLAIN faculty and students attend the sessions every other Sunday (10:00 AM US Eastern, 22:00/23:00 Beijing time), and the series has become a regular part of the lab's calendar.

Each session is announced separately in the news feed before it takes place, with the speaker, title and flyer.

Each session consists of a 20-minute talk followed by a 10-minute Q&A. Researchers interested in financial AI are welcome to join; announcements are posted through The Fin AI community channels.
