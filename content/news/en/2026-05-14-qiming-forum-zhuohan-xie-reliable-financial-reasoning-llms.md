In April 2026, **Dr. Zhuohan Xie**, a postdoctoral researcher at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) and research scientist at IFM, joined the Center for Language and Information Research for a long-term visit to advance joint projects with the lab. Dr. Xie received his PhD from the University of Melbourne, where he worked on story generation and evaluation under Jey Han Lau and Trevor Cohn; his current research, advised by Preslav Nakov, covers natural language processing, large language models, AI for finance and fact-checking. His work has appeared at ACL, EMNLP and NAACL and has been cited more than 450 times.

## Qiming Forum talk

On the afternoon of **May 14, 2026**, Dr. Xie gave a **Qiming Forum** talk in Room A213 titled **“Reliable Financial Reasoning LLMs: From Verifiable Reasoning to Multilingual Robustness”**, centred on two recent works:

- **FinChain** asks whether a model genuinely performs verifiable, constraint-consistent reasoning on financial problems rather than merely producing the right answer.
- **SAHM** examines the robustness of reasoning in multilingual and cross-cultural financial settings, and the challenges that different languages, market regimes and financial contexts pose for evaluation.

The talk argued for finer-grained evaluation that is closer to real financial scenarios as a way to understand the capability boundaries of today's financial LLMs and to build more trustworthy AI-for-finance systems.

The same afternoon, **Dr. Guojun Xiong**, postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University and Associate Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, also visited the center and met with students.
