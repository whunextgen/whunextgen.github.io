The official WeChat account of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence featured mental health language-model research led by **Prof. Qianqian Xie and Prof. Min Peng**. **Mengxi Xiao**, a PhD student in the lab, is the first author of the work, conducted with collaborators including the University of Manchester.

The feature introduced the research as **MentraSuite**. The corresponding arXiv page now uses the title **“MiraMind: Benchmarking Reliable Mental Health Reasoning beyond Answer Accuracy”**, under **arXiv:2512.09636**.

## Evaluating reasoning as well as answers

The research introduced **MentraBench**, a benchmark spanning appraisal, diagnosis, intervention, abstraction and verification. It examines how models use evidence, form judgments and organise reasoning across different tasks, extending evaluation beyond the final answer.

## Mindora reasoning models

The work also introduced **Mindora**, a family of reasoning models developed through post-training that combines supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. The report highlighted Mindora-8B's performance in the reported experiments and the readability and coherence of its reasoning.

This work builds on the lab's research in mental health NLP and language models, alongside projects such as MentaLLaMA, HealMe and MoodAngels. The School's feature brought the research on reasoning evaluation and model training to a wider university audience.
