Congratulations to PhD student **Yanlin Song**, whose paper **“MFC4POI: Multi-Factor Collaboration for Next Point-of-Interest Recommendation Using Large Language Models”** has been accepted by **Information Processing & Management (IPM)**.

Conventional next point-of-interest (POI) recommenders rely on numerical representations that obscure the semantic information in contextual features, while existing LLM-based approaches struggle to reason over multiple factors holistically. MFC4POI reformulates next-POI recommendation as a question-answering task and introduces an intent identification agent, so that a large language model can analyse a user's current behaviour from their long-term historical trajectory together with the short-term on-site situation, including dynamic preferences and nearby geography. Experiments on three real-world datasets with up to 4,000 users and 405,000 check-ins show that MFC4POI consistently outperforms strong baselines, surpassing all of them on Acc@5 and Acc@10.

Authors: Yanlin Song, Wenjie Xu, Lei Liu, Min Peng.

We thank all contributing students, advisors and collaborators for their guidance and support.
