**Prof. Sophia Ananiadou, Professor at the University of Manchester and Adjunct Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University**, is co-supervising **three funded PhD projects starting in 2027**, spanning medical AI, trustworthy multi-agent systems and AI for Science.

NaCTeM announced these opportunities on November 16, 2026. The projects are based at the University of Manchester and welcome applicants worldwide. Funding is awarded competitively.

## Projects and deadlines

| Research area | Duration | Application deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multimodal agentic AI for cardiovascular diagnosis and risk prediction | 4 years | **November 27, 2026** |
| Trustworthy multi-agent AI | 4 years | **December 4, 2026** |
| Verifiable agentic AI for atmospheric pollution | 3.5 years | **January 15, 2027** |

## 1. Multimodal agentic AI for cardiovascular care

**Reliable Multimodal Agentic AI for Cardiovascular Disease Diagnosis and Risk Prediction** will combine cardiac imaging, ECGs, blood tests and clinical records to support evidence-grounded diagnosis and risk prediction when information is incomplete or changes over time.

- **Programme:** MRC NW-DMS Doctoral Training Programme.
- **Supervisors:** Dr Jinming Duan (primary), Prof. Sophia Ananiadou and Dr Chris Miller.
- **Funding:** Four years, covering full tuition fees, including international fees, and a UKRI-rate stipend.
- **Deadline:** November 27, 2026.
- **Before applying:** Applicants must contact Dr Jinming Duan to discuss their suitability.

[Project details and application guidance](https://www.findaphd.com/phds/project/mrc-nw-dms-reliable-multimodal-agentic-ai-for-cardiovascular-disease-diagnosis-and-risk-prediction/?p201359=)

## 2. Trustworthy multi-agent AI

**Trustworthy Multi-Agent AI: Uncertainty, Alignment and Adaptive Reasoning** investigates how uncertainty and errors propagate between agents, and how evidence tracking, verification and adaptive coordination can improve reliability.

- **Programme:** UKRI AI CDT in Decision Making for Complex Systems.
- **Supervisors:** Prof. Sophia Ananiadou and Dr Mauricio Alvarez.
- **Funding:** Four years, starting September 2027. The advertised **£30,000 stipend applies to students eligible for home fee status**; international applicants should confirm their funding conditions with the project team.
- **Deadline:** December 4, 2026.

[Project details and application guidance](https://www.findaphd.com/phds/project/trustworthy-multi-agent-ai-uncertainty-alignment-and-adaptive-reasoning/?p200584=)

## 3. Verifiable agentic AI for atmospheric pollution

**Verifiable Agentic AI for Atmospheric Pollution Modelling and Prediction** explores collaborative agents for literature analysis, hypothesis development, simulation planning and verification, using atmospheric pollution and bioaerosols as applications.

- **Programme:** School of Engineering PhD Scholarships.
- **Supervisors:** Prof. Sophia Ananiadou and Prof. David Topping.
- **Funding:** 3.5 years, covering tuition fees, a UKRI minimum stipend (currently **£21,805 a year**) and up to **£2,500 a year** for research training.
- **Deadline:** January 15, 2027; expected start September 2027.
- **Background:** Previous atmospheric-science experience is not required. Contacting the supervisors before applying is recommended.

[Project details and application guidance](https://www.findaphd.com/phds/project/school-of-engineering-phd-scholarships-verifiable-agentic-ai-for-atmospheric-pollution-modelling-and-prediction/?p202836=)

## Enquiries and applications

For project 1, contact primary supervisor **Dr Jinming Duan**. For projects 2 and 3, contact **Prof. Sophia Ananiadou** at [sophia.ananiadou@manchester.ac.uk](mailto:sophia.ananiadou@manchester.ac.uk).

[Official NaCTeM announcement and project links](https://www.nactem.ac.uk/newsitem.php?item=576)

Follow each project's instructions to submit a formal University of Manchester application. An enquiry does not replace an application; eligibility, funding and deadlines remain subject to the official announcements.
