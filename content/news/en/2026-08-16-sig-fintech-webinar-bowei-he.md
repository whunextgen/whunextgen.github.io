- **Speaker:** Bowei He, MBZUAI
- **Title:** Branching Policy Optimization: Sandbox-Native Language Agent Reinforcement Learning
- **When:** Sunday, 2026-08-16, 10:00 AM US Eastern / 3:00 PM London / 22:00 Beijing time
- **Where:** Online (Zoom), part of the biweekly [ACL SIG-FinTech × TFAI Webinar Series](#/news/2026-01-18-acl-sig-fintech-tfai-webinar-series)

The talk presents Branching Policy Optimization (BPO), a reinforcement learning method for language agents in sandbox environments.

The session consists of a 20-minute talk followed by a 10-minute Q&A. Lab members attend together; researchers interested in financial AI are welcome to join through The Fin AI community channels.
- Paper: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14171>
