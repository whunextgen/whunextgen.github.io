Congratulations to three members of the lab on their success in the 2026 round of the **National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC)**:

- **Prof. Qianqian Xie** has been awarded an **NSFC General Program** grant.
- **Dr. Guojun Xiong** has been awarded an **NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C)** grant.
- **Dr. Yao Xiong** has been awarded an **NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C)** grant.

We thank all faculty and students for their continued support and look forward to building on these projects together.
