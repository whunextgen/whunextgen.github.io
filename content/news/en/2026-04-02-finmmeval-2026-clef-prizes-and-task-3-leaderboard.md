**FinMMEval 2026**, the first CLEF evaluation lab dedicated to AI for Finance, goes beyond “understanding financial language” to test models in real financial reasoning and decision-making settings. The lab is organised by MBZUAI, The Fin AI and partner institutions; CLAIN members are among the organising team, and the lab welcomes participants from academia, industry and student teams.

## Updates

- **Prizes.** Best Paper: USD 500. Outstanding Paper: USD 300 × 3. Merit / Encouragement: USD 200 × 2.
- **Task 3 leaderboard is live.** Participants deploy a system that receives daily market context for BTC and TSLA and returns Buy / Hold / Sell decisions; results are evaluated daily and updated continuously.
- **Task 3 deadline:** April 28, 2026.

## What FinMMEval evaluates

1. **Financial Exam Q&A**: multilingual exam-style multiple-choice questions on financial reasoning.
2. **Multilingual Filings and News Q&A**: evidence-grounded question answering over financial filings and news.
3. **Financial Decision Making**: decision evaluation in realistic financial contexts.

- Website: <https://mbzuai-nlp.github.io/CLEF-2026-FinMMEval-Lab/>
- CLEF 2026 will be held in Jena, Germany, on September 21–24, 2026.
