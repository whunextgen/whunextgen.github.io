On the morning of **April 20, 2026**, **Prof. Jian-Yun Nie** visited the Center for Language and Information Research at the invitation of Prof. Qianqian Xie and delivered the **26th Luojia AI Forum** lecture in Room A237 of the School of Artificial Intelligence, titled **“Retrieval-Augmented Generation: From Black Box to Open Box”**.

Prof. Nie is a professor at the Université de Montréal. He obtained his PhD from the Université de Grenoble, and his research focuses on information retrieval and natural language processing, including retrieval models, cross-lingual retrieval and, more recently, retrieval methods that use large language models. He was elected to the SIGIR Academy in 2022 and holds a Canada Research Chair in Natural Language Information Processing.

![Poster](./assets/news-20260420-nie-poster.jpg)

Large language models are increasingly used to answer questions across domains, but on their own they often hallucinate. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) offers a partial remedy, yet current approaches treat the language model as a black box: retrieved documents are placed in the prompt and the model decides how to use them through attention, with little ability to tell relevant documents from irrelevant ones. Prof. Nie presented **OpenDecoder**, an open-box approach in which signals about the quality of retrieved documents directly influence the model's attention, so that generation focuses on relevant and useful evidence and ignores noise. Experiments on several test collections show that these document-quality signals improve answer quality and make the model more robust to noisy retrieval.

Faculty and students from across the School attended the lecture and joined the discussion that followed. We thank Prof. Nie for the visit.
