Congratulations to PhD student **Yanlin Song**, whose paper **“Plan Then Retrieve: Reinforcement Learning-Guided Complex Reasoning over Knowledge Graphs”** has been accepted at **The Web Conference 2026 (WWW 2026)**.

The paper proposes **Graph-RFT**, a framework that enables large language models to autonomously plan and retrieve information from both knowledge graphs and web sources. It combines chain-of-thought fine-tuning with reinforcement learning so that the model can handle scenarios where the knowledge graph is incomplete and improve accuracy on multi-step reasoning questions.

Authors: Yanlin Song, Ben Liu, Víctor Gutiérrez-Basulto, Zhiwei Hu, Qianqian Xie, Min Peng, Sophia Ananiadou, Jeff Z. Pan.

We thank all contributing students, advisors and collaborators for their guidance and support.
