On December 18, 2025, the technology media outlet **TechWalker** published a feature article titled *“Wuhan University team tackles the web novel translation challenge: how to make AI truly understand cultural connotation and character emotion”*, introducing **DITING**, the web novel translation evaluation framework developed by our lab.

DITING is the first comprehensive evaluation system designed specifically for web novel translation. It defines **six evaluation dimensions** and provides more than **18,000 expert-annotated Chinese–English sentence pairs**. Alongside the benchmark, the team proposed **AgentEval**, a multi-agent evaluation framework that simulates expert discussion to judge translation quality. AgentEval achieves a correlation of **0.669** with human judgement, exceeding all existing automatic metrics.

The work was carried out by researchers from the School of Artificial Intelligence and the Center for Language and Information Research at Wuhan University under the joint supervision of **Prof. Qianqian Xie**, **Prof. Min Peng** and **Prof. Sophia Ananiadou** (University of Manchester).
