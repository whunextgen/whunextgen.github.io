- **Speaker:** Shuzhi Gong, University of Melbourne
- **Title:** Multi-Sourced, Multi-Agent Evidence Retrieval for Fact-Checking
- **When:** Sunday, 2026-08-02, 10:00 AM US Eastern / 3:00 PM London / 22:00 Beijing time
- **Where:** Online (Zoom), part of the biweekly [ACL SIG-FinTech × TFAI Webinar Series](#/news/2026-01-18-acl-sig-fintech-tfai-webinar-series)

Based on the speaker's SIGIR 2026 full paper on multi-source, multi-agent evidence retrieval for fact-checking.

The session consists of a 20-minute talk followed by a 10-minute Q&A. Lab members attend together; researchers interested in financial AI are welcome to join through The Fin AI community channels.
- Paper: <https://doi.org/10.1145/3805712.3809686>
