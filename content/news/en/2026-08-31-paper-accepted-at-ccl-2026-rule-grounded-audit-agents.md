Congratulations to **Maowei Jiang**, whose paper **“Seeing Is Not Knowing: A Benchmark for Rule-Grounded Audit Agents”** has been accepted at **the 25th China National Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL 2026)**.

The paper introduces a benchmark for audit agents that must ground their judgements in explicit rules rather than surface observations.

We thank all contributing students, advisors and collaborators for their guidance and support.
