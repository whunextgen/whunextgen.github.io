- **Speaker:** Ye Yuan, McGill University / Mila
- **Title:** SPINiverse: Using Internal Signals of AI Models for Better and Faster Decisions
- **When:** Sunday, 2026-09-13, 10:00 AM US Eastern / 3:00 PM London / 22:00 Beijing time
- **Where:** Online (Zoom), part of the biweekly [ACL SIG-FinTech × TFAI Webinar Series](#/news/2026-01-18-acl-sig-fintech-tfai-webinar-series)

The talk explores how internal signals of AI models can make classification, AI safety, document retrieval and agent monitoring more efficient and effective.

The session consists of a 20-minute talk followed by a 10-minute Q&A. Lab members attend together; researchers interested in financial AI are welcome to join through The Fin AI community channels.
