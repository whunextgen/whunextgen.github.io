The **first CCF Large Model and AI Engineering Conference and NLP Strategy Symposium** published its programme for **June 11–13, 2026 in Tianjin**. Organised by the China Computer Federation, the programme connected large-model research, AI engineering and industry applications.

The official speaker poster lists **Prof. Qianqian Xie** of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence among the invited participants in the **AI Healthcare Forum**, scheduled for **June 11, 14:00–17:30**.

## AI in healthcare

The forum formed part of the conference's industry programme. Prof. Xie's research in natural language processing and medical AI includes domain knowledge, language models, reasoning and evaluation.

The published agenda provides the forum topic and invited-speaker information. The lower half of the official poster below shows the AI Healthcare Forum and includes Prof. Xie.

## Connecting research and engineering

The conference programme also included technical, industry and frontier-research forums, doctoral posters, AI system demonstrations and opportunities for academic–industry exchange. These activities brought together model research, engineering and application requirements.
