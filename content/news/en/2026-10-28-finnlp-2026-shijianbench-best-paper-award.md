The **11th Workshop on Financial Technology and Natural Language Processing (FinNLP 2026)** took place in **Budapest, Hungary, on 28 October 2026**, alongside EMNLP 2026. CLAIN's collaborative work **ShiJianBench received a Domyn Best Paper Award**. Prof. Qianqian Xie gave an invited talk, and Prof. Sophia Ananiadou served as a General Chair and delivered the opening remarks.

FinNLP brings together research on financial text understanding, language agents, information retrieval, shared tasks and trustworthy evaluation. Its programme connects methodological developments with questions arising in financial applications through talks, posters, oral presentations and discussion.

## Domyn Best Paper Award for ShiJianBench

**“ShiJianBench: From Dialogue to Decision for Long-Horizon Evaluation of Investment Advisors,”** first-authored by PhD student **Jie Gong**, with Maowei Jiang, Zhiwei Liu and other collaborators, was presented in the oral programme and received a **Domyn Best Paper Award**.

The study examines how conversational investment advisors influence users' subsequent decisions. Financial conversations unfold as markets and investor states change, so the quality of an individual response offers limited evidence about the longer-term effects of an advisory service. The research therefore brings advisor language, evolving investor states and decision trajectories into a common evaluation framework.

**ShiJianBench** introduces a multi-agent investor simulator with evolving states, long-term memory and dialogue-grounded updates. Advisor policies are compared under fixed historical market feedback using investor-side, service-side and content-side metrics, alongside a compliance gate.

The results distinguish response quality from longer-term decision outcomes. Fluent, personalized advice and effective support over a sustained interaction require separate evaluation. The work provides a framework for studying the behavior of financial agents and the effects of conversational interventions over time.

## Qianqian Xie Shares Research on Long-Horizon Alignment

Prof. Qianqian Xie delivered the invited talk **“Beyond Response Quality: From Financial Simulation to Long-Horizon Alignment of Investment Advisory Agents,”** discussing financial simulation and the long-horizon alignment of investment advisory agents.

The topic extends evaluation from response quality to sustained interaction: as language models enter advisory services, assessment needs to consider generated content, the continuing interaction with a user and changes in that user's decisions over time. Financial simulation and trajectory-based evaluation provide experimental settings for analyzing these questions across advisor policies.

Prof. Xie also joined the panel discussion on the role and public value of financial NLP. The discussion considered research goals, application needs and beneficiaries, including the accessibility of complex financial information and the problem of information asymmetry.

## Further Collaborative Work in the Poster Programme

The collaborative paper **“FinEngage: Benchmarking Multimodal Language Models on Content-Conditioned Human Feedback Inference”** was also listed in the poster programme.

The work focuses on content-conditioned human feedback inference, adding an evaluation perspective on multimodal financial content and user feedback. Together with the longer-term decision processes studied by ShiJianBench, these research directions examine different connections between model outputs and human understanding, feedback and behavior.

**FinEngage authors (in paper order):** Qiqi Qiang, Ying Peng, Yunmiao Zhang, Zhiwei Liu, Victor Gutierrez Basulto, Yifei Zhang, Dejun Cao, Jie Gong, Min Peng, Honghai Yu, Qianqian Xie.
