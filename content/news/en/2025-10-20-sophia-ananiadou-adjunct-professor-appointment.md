Wuhan University has appointed **Prof. Sophia Ananiadou** of the University of Manchester as an **adjunct professor** for the period **October 2025 to October 2028**. The appointment letter is dated October 20, 2025.

The appointment supports international collaboration in natural language processing, text mining and artificial intelligence at the School of Artificial Intelligence and the Center for Language and Information Research, including joint research and exchanges with faculty and students.

## Text mining and domain AI

Prof. Ananiadou's research focuses on text mining and biomedical natural language processing. She is a professor at the University of Manchester and Director of the UK's National Centre for Text Mining (NaCTeM). Her work examines how knowledge can be extracted, organised and synthesised from large text collections for scientific and professional workflows.

The appointment supports collaboration through the Center for Language and Information Research on knowledge-aware AI systems and cross-document scientific knowledge modelling, alongside the joint training of students.
