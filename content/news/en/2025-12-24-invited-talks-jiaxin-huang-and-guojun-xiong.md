On December 24 and 25, 2025, the **Language and Information Center lecture series** welcomed two invited speakers to the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University.

## From 3D to 4D Spatial Reasoning

**Jiaxin Huang**, a second-year PhD student at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), advised by Prof. Tongliang Liu, Prof. Mingming Gong and Prof. Xue Liu, gave a talk titled **“From 3D to 4D Spatial Reasoning”** on December 24 in Room A237. The talk presented a line of work on three- and four-dimensional scene understanding: **UrbanGS**, which models dynamic urban scenes with 4D Gaussian Splatting; **MLLM-For3D**, which transfers the reasoning ability of 2D multimodal large language models to language-guided 3D reasoning segmentation without 3D annotations; and the **Surprise3D** dataset, which systematically evaluates spatial reasoning in complex 3D scenes through carefully designed language queries.

## Structured Reinforcement Learning for Decision Making under Uncertainty

**Dr. Guojun Xiong**, a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Computer Science at Harvard University who will join Shanghai Jiao Tong University as a tenure-track Associate Professor, spoke on December 25 in Room A213. His research addresses complex, resource-constrained sequential decision problems under uncertainty by developing structured reinforcement learning frameworks that exploit problem structure to improve sample efficiency and learning speed. Topics covered included online sequential decision making under uncertainty, stochastic optimization and control, finite-time convergence and regret analysis of reinforcement learning, and decentralized optimization and multi-agent reinforcement learning for networked decision systems. He also discussed the broader impact of his work with the Teamcore group at Harvard on public health and social good.

Both talks were followed by lively discussion with faculty and students. We thank the speakers for sharing their work with the lab.
