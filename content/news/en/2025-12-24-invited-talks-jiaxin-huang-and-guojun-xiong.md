On December 24, 2025, **Jiaxin Huang**, a PhD student at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), visited the Center for Language and Information Research and gave a **Qiming Forum** talk titled **“From 3D to 4D Spatial Reasoning.”**

## From static scenes to dynamic spatial reasoning

The talk traced the development of static 3D scene understanding towards dynamic 4D modelling. It introduced **UrbanGS** for dynamic urban scenes, **MLLM-For3D** for transferring multimodal language-model reasoning to 3D tasks, and **Surprise3D**, a dataset for evaluating spatial reasoning in complex scenes.

Together, these projects connected scene representation, method design and evaluation: how models understand spatial relationships, handle changes over time and reason from language instructions. The discussion offered lab members a perspective on the relationship between world models, embodied intelligence and multimodal understanding.

Huang's research covers world models, embodied intelligence, 3D/4D scene reconstruction and understanding, and medical image analysis. Her doctoral advisors are Prof. Tongliang Liu, Prof. Mingming Gong and Prof. Xue Liu. During the visit, she exchanged research ideas with faculty and students.
