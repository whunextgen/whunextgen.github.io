The **CCF–Shenzhen Loop Area Institute event** took place in Shenzhen on February 1–2, 2026 under the theme **“Agentic Intelligence for Human–AI Society.”** Its programme included research talks, thematic forums and doctoral exchanges on agents, human–AI collaboration and interdisciplinary AI research.

On **February 2, Prof. Qianqian Xie** of Wuhan University's School of Artificial Intelligence spoke in the **AI for Social Sciences** forum. The organiser's programme gives the title **“LLM-Driven Intelligent Finance: Benchmarks, Models, Multi-Agent Systems, and Applications.”**

## Language models and financial decisions

The presentation connected benchmarks, domain models, multi-agent systems and financial applications. Financial tasks pose questions about the use of professional knowledge, the interpretation of information and the reasoning required for analysis and decisions.

The organiser's event recap describes Prof. Xie's contribution to the discussion of AI capabilities in intelligent financial decision making. These topics connect with the lab's work on financial NLP, language-model evaluation and agents, and provide a setting for discussing model capabilities alongside application requirements.

## Interdisciplinary exchange

The AI for Social Sciences forum brought together model research and questions from finance, economics and other professional domains. Participants discussed research approaches and the different ways disciplines frame tasks, evidence and methods.
